# <The idea being shaped>

## Problem

> Who has the problem, what they cannot do today, and how we will know it is solved.

## Sources

> What you read, what you did not read, what you inferred, and what is missing.

## Vocabulary

> The words we will use for the new concepts (and words we will not use), so code, UI and docs agree.

## Options

> Two or more ways to do it, each with what it costs and what it rules out.

## Questions to decide

> One per line, each with a recommended answer and who decides.

## Candidate slices

> Each slice must ship something usable on its own; later slices must not break earlier ones; things
> that cannot be added later go first.

## Recommendation

> What to do next, and what has to be true before starting.
